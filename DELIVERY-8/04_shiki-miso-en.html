The History of Fuji no Sato Kobo

In the year 2000, local farming mothers gathered in the hidden valley of Miyoshi, Tokushima, to form "Fuji no Sato Kobo"—a sanctuary dedicated to passing down the beautiful culinary culture of the countryside to future generations. With roots tracing back to a 1970s lifestyle improvement group, we stubbornly preserve our additive-free traditional methods as the truest form of love for our hometown.

The soul of the miso, koji (fermentation starter), is made entirely from scratch by hand. By blending barley miso and rice miso in a perfect ratio, this craft miso is designed to elegantly draw out the gentle, natural sweetness of the ingredients. Aged slowly in wooden barrels alongside the "winds of the four seasons" that sweep through this region of extreme temperature shifts, our miso possesses a clean, rich fermentation power that aligns the body and mind from the core.





5 Wild Superfoods — A Recipe of Life

Woven into this exquisite fermented base are five wild superfoods—the very vitality these plants have stored within themselves to survive the harsh wilderness of the hidden valley.

[ Wasabi ]
Brassicaceae family (Perennial). A highly delicate plant unique to Japan, growing only along harsh mountain streams flowing with exceptionally pure spring water. Its exhilarating, nose-tingling sharpness (allyl isothiocyanate) sharply awakens the five senses, possessing strong antibacterial properties and the power to cleanly regulate the body's circulation.

[ Sansho (Japanese Pepper) ]
Rutaceae family (Deciduous shrub). A resilient plant growing wild on steep mountain slopes. Characterized by its electrifying, citrus-tinged sharpness (sanshool) that tingles the tongue, it activates organ functions, warms the body from the core, and gently regulates your physical condition.

[ Fukinoto (Giant Butterbur Sprout) ]
Asteraceae family (Perennial). The "messenger of spring" that first emerges through the thawing snow of freezing winters. Characterized by a powerful wild bitterness (polyphenols and alkaloids), it contains the wisdom of detox—clearing away the heaviness accumulated during winter and gently awakening the body for spring.

[ Chestnut ]
Fagaceae family (Deciduous tree). A symbol of the hidden valley's rich autumn mountains, chestnuts have been a clean energy source supporting Japanese lives since ancient times. Within its comforting, earthy sweetness lies an abundance of vitamins, dietary fiber, and highly antioxidant tannins, slowly replenishing the vitality needed for daily life.

[ Yuzu ]
Rutaceae family (Evergreen shrub). Bearing vibrant golden fruit during the valley's winters of extreme temperature shifts. Characterized by an essential-oil-like, glamorous, and nostalgic citrus aroma, along with abundant Vitamin C. It brings a warm moisture to the heart during frozen seasons, boosting immunity and fortifying the body's natural barriers.

We insist on using 100% locally sourced, seasonal wild ingredients, ensuring their inherent gentle sweetness and profound umami take center stage. Crafted entirely without preservatives or chemical seasonings, it is an authentic slow food that fuses overwhelming deliciousness with the medicinal properties of wild nature—a true joy for both body and mind.
